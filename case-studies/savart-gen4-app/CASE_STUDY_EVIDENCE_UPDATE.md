### Research foundation

The product document states that the shortcomings of the existing application were identified after interviews with **close to 100 customers, internal stakeholders, and potential prospects**.

The recurring needs represented in the documented personas are:

- Limited investment knowledge
- Low confidence in investment decisions
- Insufficient time for research
- Difficulty balancing risk and diversification
- Emotional or speculative investment behavior
- Need for personalized guidance and ongoing support

### Primary personas

1. **A first-time investor** who understands the value of long-term investing but needs education and guidance.
2. **A cautious existing investor** who wants to diversify beyond mutual funds while managing risk.
3. **An experienced but emotionally affected investor** who wants to move away from speculation toward evidence-led, long-term decisions.

### Confirmed product problem

Savart needed to transform an existing customer application into a connected investment experience for people who lacked the time, confidence, or knowledge to manage investing independently.

The product had to:

- Collect enough financial and behavioral information for meaningful personalization without overwhelming the user.
- Make recommendations and portfolio intelligence understandable rather than presenting advice as a black box.
- Connect advice to broker execution and ongoing portfolio monitoring.
- Build trust around financial-data access, consent, projections, external providers, regulatory boundaries, failures, and incomplete information.
- Reduce dependence on customer-support teams through walkthroughs, simulations, contextual help, and self-service support.

## Constraints and risks that can be discussed

### Regulatory and trust

- SEBI/RBI privacy and security requirements
- Compliance questions around free reviews and personalized recommendations
- Investment projections potentially being interpreted as guaranteed returns
- Need for clear consent and external-provider boundaries
- Need to distinguish prospect and subscriber experiences

### Data and integrations

- Account Aggregator information could be incomplete or inconsistent
- Broker holdings could become stale or require session renewal
- Incorrect source data could affect portfolio analysis
- External providers create redirects, authentication, latency, and recovery states

### Product and operational scale

- The product covers many interconnected services and user stages
- Guidance content must remain synchronized with product changes
- Chatbot accuracy directly affects trust and adoption
- Reward systems introduce fraud, expiry, reversal, and abuse-handling requirements
- Some pricing, integrations, and feature rules were still undecided during planning
